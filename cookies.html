Cookie Policy

How this website uses browser preferences and related technologies.

Industrial preview notice revised 11 November 2026. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is optional and disabled until you choose “Accept analytics”.

1. Essential browser storage

OnChainMake uses essential website functionality and local browser storage to remember your privacy selection. A preference record may be saved in localStorage as ocm_cookie_consent_v1 with your choice, policy version and selection timestamp. It is not an advertising identifier and does not include your name or email. The old essential-only preference key ocm_cookie_preference_v1 is not treated as analytics consent.

2. Optional Google Analytics 4

If you choose Accept analytics, this industrial website loads Google Analytics 4 with Measurement ID G-XLG2N4EMG5 to understand visits, page views and website usage. GA4 may set analytics cookies, including _ga and _ga_*, and send usage information to Google. Google Analytics may process information outside your country. We do not use this integration for Google Ads personalisation, and the Google tag is not loaded before analytics consent.

The industrial site uses the additional GA4 event parameter site_variant=industrial_v8_2. The existing public landing uses the same Measurement ID; reporting requires suitable segmentation in GA4.

3. Other services

Cloudflare provides public website hosting and delivery. This industrial website uses locally hosted fonts and images rather than optional external font services. Any separate linked service, including the member application, may have its own privacy and cookie practices; its presence in a link does not mean its cookies are set by this page. If new optional services are enabled, this notice and the relevant controls will require review.

4. Manage or withdraw your choice

Select Reject optional or close the banner to keep GA4 disabled. Select Accept analytics to enable it. To review, change or withdraw consent at any time, open cookie preferences, also available in every page footer. If you withdraw consent after enabling analytics, this site updates its consent state, attempts to remove accessible GA4 cookies for this domain and reloads the page without loading GA4. You can additionally remove cookies and site data in browser settings. Your analytics consent is stored separately for each website origin.

5. Contact

Questions about cookies can be sent to the website's individual operator Lorenzo Michele Gassani Benedetti at info@onchainmake.com, or by certified email (Italian PEC) at lorenzogassanibenedetti@pec.it.
