Cookie Policy

Version 1.2 · Effective 30 July 2026 · Applies to: onchainmake.com, app.onchainmake.com and related pilot services

This Cookie Policy explains how OnChainMake uses cookies and similar technologies on its website and pilot application.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage, session storage, SDKs, tracking tags and browser identifiers.

We use cookies to operate the site, maintain security, remember preferences, measure usage, support access requests and improve the pilot experience.

1. Categories of Cookies

1.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These are required for security, navigation, authentication, session management, consent management and basic website functionality. They cannot be disabled through our cookie tools.

1.2 Functional cookies

These remember preferences such as cookie choices, form progress, language, display settings or login-related preferences.

1.3 Analytics cookies

These help us understand how visitors use the website and pilot application, including which pages are visited, how users navigate and where improvements are needed.

On the public website, we use Google Analytics 4 with Measurement ID G-XLG2N4EMG5. The Google Analytics tag is blocked by default and is loaded only after you select Accept analytics. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google signals remain disabled in this website configuration.

When analytics is accepted, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visits and sessions. Cookie names, technical details and durations may be changed by the provider or limited by your browser.

1.4 CRM and access request cookies

These may support access forms, pilot onboarding, communication tracking, HubSpot or similar CRM workflows and follow-up communications.

1.5 Security cookies

These help detect abuse, bots, suspicious traffic, unauthorised access and technical incidents.

2. Cookies Used During the Pilot

During the pilot we may use cookies and similar technologies from OnChainMake, hosting and security providers, authentication providers, analytics providers, CRM or access request tools, form providers, and wallet connection or application infrastructure tools where applicable.

The public website stores your cookie preference in local browser storage under ocm_cookie_consent_v1. This preference is used only to remember whether analytics is enabled or disabled. It does not contain your name, email address or account credentials.

Specific cookie names and durations may change as the pilot evolves.

3. Consent

Where required by law, we request consent before setting non-essential cookies. On the public website, analytics is off by default. Selecting Reject optional or closing the banner keeps analytics disabled. Selecting Accept analytics enables Google Analytics 4.

You may withdraw or change your choice at any time through . If analytics was previously enabled and you disable it, the website updates the consent state, removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this domain and reloads the page with analytics blocked.

Blocking some cookies may affect site functionality, access requests, login, security or pilot application features.

4. Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to block, delete or manage cookies. If you disable cookies, some parts of the website or pilot application may not work correctly.

5. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy as the website, pilot application, analytics, CRM tools or platform functionality changes.
