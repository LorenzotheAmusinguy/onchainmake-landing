We aim to apply proportionate safeguards such as access restrictions, encrypted transmission, authentication controls, audit trails and appropriate handling of confidential industrial files. No public website visitor should provide private keys, seed phrases, live payment instructions or confidential manufacturing files through an unsecured contact form. Demo / Mock transactions do not involve real economic settlement. A personal data breach will be assessed and notified to individuals or authorities when required by the applicable law; no blanket 72-hour promise is made for every incident.
