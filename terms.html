6. Capacity Slots and Reservations

Capacity slots represent production capacity made available by a Maker under defined parameters. A capacity slot may remain visible while residual capacity is available. A slot may be partially consumed by one or more orders, depending on platform configuration. The Platform may calculate residual capacity using order, reservation and administrative data.

During the pilot, capacity slot data, residual capacity, reservation status and availability may be managed through a combination of database logic, platform workflows, manual review and selected smart contract or token-ready structures.

A capacity slot does not guarantee final order acceptance. Makers may still need to review technical feasibility, scheduling, compliance, commercial terms and milestone conditions before accepting an order.
