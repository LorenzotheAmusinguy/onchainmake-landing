The order lifecycle

Five connected decisions.

01

Start with capacity

Manufacturers identify usable production capacity; Buyers select a suitable starting point and provide their scope of work.

02

Agree requirements and milestones

Define pricing, delivery, acceptance criteria, milestones, technical evidence and commercial responsibilities.

03

Fund the agreement

Agreed amounts and release terms are established in the applicable escrow configuration before production advances.

04

Produce and verify

Manufacturers share progress evidence and completed work is assessed against the agreed technical criteria.

05

Approve and settle

Once the agreed conditions are satisfied, the order advances toward settlement. Detailed release conditions are set out in the Terms of Service.

Trust has conditions

No positive verification.
No release.

Milestone-based escrow is designed around evidence and authorization. Unresolved disputes block the affected payment release, and repeated payout is prohibited by the settlement design.
