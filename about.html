About OnChainMake

We know the challenges behind every custom order.

OnChainMake was born from operational experience—and a desire to make industrial Make-to-Order work simpler, clearer and more reliable for both sides.

Our story ↓ Contact us →
Where it started

We came from Operations. We built for Operations.

Lorenzo and Renato know the pressure behind Make-to-Order manufacturing. A purchase order is only the beginning: capacity has to be available, requirements must be clear, people need to coordinate, and every milestone matters.

Too often, those moving parts are managed across disconnected conversations, documents and systems. We wanted to give buyers and manufacturers a more practical way to work together—from the first commitment to the final payment.

Built around the realities of manufacturing.
Why we created OnChainMake

Less friction between order and execution.

Our goal is not to replace a company's ERP or the expertise of its people. It is to help connect the commitments that matter most in Make-to-Order work.

01

Capacity with context.

Give manufacturing availability a clearer role in the buyer–manufacturer conversation.

02

Execution without guesswork.

Keep requirements, production milestones and progress easier to understand across both teams.

03

Trust that supports cash flow.

Connect verifiable progress with transparent, condition-based payment arrangements.

Our perspective

Technology should solve operational problems—not create new ones.

That is the thinking behind OnChainMake: an industrial platform designed around how Make-to-Order relationships actually work, with a focus on clarity, accountability and practical value.
