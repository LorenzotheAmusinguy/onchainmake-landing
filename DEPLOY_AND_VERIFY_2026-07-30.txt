UPLOAD
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the GitHub repository.
3. Choose Add file > Upload files.
4. Drag the CONTENTS of the extracted folder into GitHub. Do not upload the outer folder as a new nested directory.
5. Commit message: Add GA4 with consent controls
6. Commit to main. Cloudflare will deploy automatically.

VERIFY AFTER CLOUDFLARE SHOWS SUCCESS
1. Open https://onchainmake.com in a private/incognito window.
2. The privacy banner must appear.
3. Before accepting, browser cookies must not contain _ga or _ga_*.
4. Select Accept analytics.
5. Reload the page and navigate to buyer.html and maker.html.
6. Confirm Google Analytics Realtime shows the visit.
7. Select Cookie settings in the footer, then Reject optional.
8. The page reloads and _ga / _ga_* cookies should be removed.
9. Verify the website still loads correctly on desktop and mobile.

ROLLBACK
If the site does not work, use Cloudflare Pages > onchainmake-landing > Deployments and roll back to production commit cfabc36.

DO NOT
- Do not touch the onchainmakemvp Cloudflare project.
- Do not change DNS or custom domains.
- Do not add the Google tag a second time through Google Tag Manager.
